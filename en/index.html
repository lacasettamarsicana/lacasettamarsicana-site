Explore

Discover Cappadocia and Marsica

Marsica is a land of mountains, woods, villages and wide-open spaces, ideal for anyone who loves nature in every season. From Cappadocia you can combine days in the snow, walks through trails and beech woods, high-altitude hikes and visits to places rich in history and charm.

La Casetta Marsicana is a convenient base for discovering Tagliacozzo, Camporotondo, the Simbruini and Carseolani Mountains and some of the most interesting destinations in western Marsica.

Things to see around Cappadocia

  • TagliacozzoA historic town in Marsica, ideal for a stroll through its old centre, squares, historic buildings and local venues.
  • Camporotondo ski areaA mountain resort in the municipality of Cappadocia, dedicated to winter sports and offering access to the woods of the Simbruini Mountains.
  • Sentiero dei BrigantiA hiking route inspired by the history of brigandage in the Abruzzo Apennines, inviting visitors to discover the area at a slower pace.
  • Alba Fucens archaeological siteAn ancient Roman colony on the slopes of Monte Velino, with walls, roads and monumental remains set in the Marsican landscape.
  • Cammino di San TommasoThe pilgrimage route between Rome and Ortona also passes through Cappadocia, with a stage connecting Monte Livata, Camporotondo and Marsica.
  • Beatrice Cenci CavesA karst cave complex in the Cappadocia area, between Petrella Liri and Verrecchie, with large underground chambers and natural formations.
  • Monte VelinoOne of the major peaks of the central Apennines and a landmark for hiking and panoramic views in the Sirente-Velino Park area.
  • Monte CamiciolaA hiking destination in the Carseolani Mountains, reached via routes starting from the Camporotondo area.
  • Monte PadiglioneA peak of the Carseolani Mountains above Verrecchie, known for ridge walks and broad views across the surrounding landscape.
  • Santissima Trinità SanctuaryA historic sanctuary in Vallepietra, set beneath the cliffs of Monte Autore and also reachable from the Camporotondo side.
