Cookie Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website may store in a user's browser to enable certain functions, remember preferences or collect information about browsing activity.

2. Cookies used by this website

In its current configuration, La Casetta Marsicana does not use profiling or advertising cookies, nor analytics tools designed to track user behaviour for commercial purposes.

The technical infrastructure that hosts and protects the website may, where strictly necessary, use technical security or traffic-management tools. La Casetta Marsicana does not use these tools for advertising or profiling purposes.

3. Google Maps

The map displayed on the website is a static image hosted locally and does not load Google content during normal page viewing. The “Open in Google Maps” button is simply an external link: only after clicking it does the user leave this website and access Google services, which are subject to their own terms and privacy notices.

4. Third-party cookies

In its current configuration, the website does not embed third-party services that require prior consent for profiling or marketing purposes. If analytics tools, embedded content or other services using non-essential trackers are added in the future, this policy will be updated and, where required, a consent mechanism will be introduced.

5. Managing cookies in your browser

You can still control, block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling any strictly necessary technical cookies may affect some security features or the correct delivery of websites in general.

6. Controller and contact details

Data controller: Luca Fizzarotti, private management of La Casetta Marsicana. For privacy-related requests, you can write to info@lacasettamarsicana.it.

For more information about the processing of personal data, please see the Privacy Policy.
