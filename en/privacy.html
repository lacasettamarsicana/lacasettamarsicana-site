Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

1. Data controller

The data controller for personal data collected through this website is Luca Fizzarotti, in connection with the private management of La Casetta Marsicana.

Email contact: info@lacasettamarsicana.it.

2. Data processed while browsing

During normal browsing, the IT systems and services used to make this website available may collect technical data such as IP address, browser type, request time and other information required for the operation, security and correct delivery of the pages.

The website is hosted through Cloudflare infrastructure. This data may therefore be processed by the technical provider for purposes strictly related to content delivery, security and service protection.

3. Data provided voluntarily

The website does not use contact forms that directly store personal data. If you choose to email the address shown on the website, the sender's address, the content of the message and any other information you choose to provide will be processed.

4. Purposes and legal basis

  • operating and securing the website;
  • responding to requests for information and availability;
  • handling pre-contractual requests relating to a stay;
  • complying with any legal obligations connected with a subsequent booking or guest relationship.

Depending on the circumstances, requests submitted by users are processed in order to take pre-contractual steps requested by the data subject and to comply with legal obligations. Technical processing required for security and correct operation of the website is carried out on the basis of the controller's legitimate interest in keeping the service available and secure.

5. Recipients of data

Data may be processed by technical providers required for the operation of the website and email service, within the limits of their respective functions. Data may also be disclosed to competent authorities where required by law.

6. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Some technical providers may operate outside the European Economic Area. Where this occurs, transfers are carried out using the mechanisms provided for by Regulation (EU) 2016/679 and under the applicable terms of the individual provider.

7. Retention

Technical data is retained for the time necessary for the security and operation of the service. Email communications are retained for the time needed to respond to and manage the request; they may be retained for longer where a booking relationship begins or where necessary to comply with legal obligations or protect rights.

8. Data subject rights

Where provided for by law, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and you may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You can exercise your rights by writing to info@lacasettamarsicana.it.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

9. External services and links

The map shown on the main page is a static image hosted on this website and does not embed Google Maps. The “Open in Google Maps” link instead takes you to an external service; once opened, data processing is governed by the terms and privacy policy of that provider.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the website, the services used or legal requirements change. The version published on this page is the current version.

For information about cookies and similar technologies, please also see the Cookie Policy.
